Intro / About Me

I'm Varshitha, but I go by different names. My friends sometimes use "Varsh" as a nickname, and I also go by "Sri" among some classmates or professors. My name means rain, and I live in Seattle (coincidence?).

I plan to use these pages to recount anything that's happened in my life, maybe once a month or so at the end of each month? I'll probably do a different page for May, separate from this short intro. I'm not sure how long each "blog post" will be, or how much detail I feel comfortable sharing online. However, I think it'll be fun to write about myself in a different context than I have previously.

I'm about to graduate from university, I picked up my stole this week. It feels strange to close this chapter, especially because I've been here since I was 15. You could argue that I've been here since I was 14, even, if you count Transition School. I'm 20 now (that's 6 years!!). I feel like I've grown and changed a lot in that time, especially recently.

Despite everything going on in my personal life and within the world, I like to think that I'm an optimistic and generally pretty happy person. Therefore, I believe that I will continue growing and changing for the better over the coming months/years. I hope I can document my personal and professional growth here to look back on in the future.
